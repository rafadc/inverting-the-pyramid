Prologue

What's the difference?

A tapas bar in Lisbon, Euro 2004. A journalist asks why anyone cares whether England play 4-4-2 or a diamond. Wilson's answer is the whole book: the numbers are not the same, and the shape decides where the game is won.

The flat 4-4-2

Two banks of four. Compact, easy to coach, and the shape Sven-Göran Eriksson trusted. The width comes from the wide midfielders; the centre has two bodies.

England

The diamond

Same eleven players, four midfielders still, but now one sits, two shuttle and one plays behind the strikers. The centre is crowded, the flanks are empty. Which one you pick decides who gets the ball where.

The pyramid, inverted

Football began with two defenders and five forwards. It ends the book with five defenders and one. Numbers, it turns out, are everything.
